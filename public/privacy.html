Privacy policy: Ravi tools

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Ravi tools is a personal app. Its only user is its owner, Ravi Gupta, who uses it to read and manage his own YouTube channel, Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive.

What data it uses

Only data from the owner's own Google account that he authorizes: YouTube channel and video statistics, video details and captions; and, read-only, his email, calendar events and Drive files.

How it is used

The data is processed only on the owner's own computers, to report on and manage his own accounts. It is not sold, shared with or shown to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Ravi tools' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
